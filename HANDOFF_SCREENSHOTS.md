## 📸 HOW TO READ SCREENSHOTS — EXACT METHOD

**When Hirthick sends screenshots, always do this in order:**

### Step 1 — Identify what you're looking at
- What timeframe? (M1, M5, M15, H1, H4)
- What pair? (XAU/USD, GBP/USD, BTC/USD)
- What time is it in Kaunas? (check chart timestamp, add hours if needed)
- Is it London session, NY session, or Asian (avoid)?

### Step 2 — Read the Moving Averages
Look for TWO red curved lines on the chart (EMA9 and EMA21):
- **Fast MA (EMA9)** — reacts quickly, follows price closely
- **Slow MA (EMA21)** — smoother, lags behind

Ask yourself:
- Is price ABOVE both MAs? → Bullish bias
- Is price BELOW both MAs? → Bearish bias
- Is price BETWEEN them? → Indecision, be careful
- Did fast MA cross above slow MA? → Bullish momentum shift
- Did fast MA cross below slow MA? → Bearish momentum shift
- Are MAs sloping steeply? → Strong trend
- Are MAs flat and tangled? → Ranging market, avoid

### Step 3 — Read the Candles
Look at the last 5-10 candles:

**Color:**
- Green candle = close higher than open (buyers won)
- Red candle = close lower than open (sellers won)
- 3+ same color in a row = real momentum

**Body size:**
- Large body = strong conviction, big move
- Small body = weak, indecision
- Tiny doji = complete indecision — wait for next candle

**Wicks:**
- Long upper wick = price tried to go up but got rejected → bearish signal at resistance
- Long lower wick = price tried to go down but bounced → bullish signal at support
- Clean body, no wicks = pure momentum in that direction

**Patterns to look for:**
- 3 green candles after support hold → buy setup forming
- Long upper wick at resistance → sell setup forming
- Doji at key level → wait — next candle decides
- Big red candle after extended rally → reversal warning

### Step 4 — Identify Key Levels
Scan the chart for:
- **Previous highs** — where price topped out and rejected
- **Previous lows** — where price bounced from
- **Round numbers** — 4,200 / 4,150 / 4,100 on Gold, 1.3300 on GBP
- **EMA9 level** — acts as dynamic support/resistance
- **The ask/bid lines** — red dotted and teal dotted lines showing current price

Mark these mentally:
- Nearest resistance above current price
- Nearest support below current price
- Is price AT a level or floating between levels?
- **AT a level = potential setup**
- **Between levels = no trade, wait**

### Step 5 — Count Confirmations (need 3 of 5 to trade)

| # | Check | Bullish ✅ | Bearish ✅ |
|---|-------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | MA position | Price above EMA9 | Price below EMA9 |

| 3 | Candle color | 3+ green candles | 3+ red candles |
| 4 | Structure | Higher highs + higher lows | Lower highs + lower lows |
| 5 | Level | At support, rejecting down | At resistance, rejecting up |

- 0-2 → ❌ NO TRADE
- 3 → ⚠️ Possible — set limit order, wait for price to come to you
- 4-5 → ✅ HIGH CONFIDENCE — place it

## 📚 ICT CONCEPTS REFERENCE

| Term | What it means | How to spot it on chart |
|------|--------------|------------------------|
| **CHoCH** | Change of Character — trend about to flip | Price makes first lower high (in uptrend) or higher low (in downtrend) |
| **BOS** | Break of Structure — trend confirmed | Price closes beyond previous swing high/low |
| **FVG** | Fair Value Gap — price moved too fast, left a gap | Three candles where middle candle body doesn't overlap wicks of candles 1 and 3 |
| **IFVG** | Inverse FVG — filled FVG becomes support/resistance | Old FVG zone that price has since traded through |
| **BPR** | Balanced Price Range — two overlapping FVGs | Narrow zone where two FVGs overlap = strong level |
| **Liquidity sweep** | Price hunts stops below support/above resistance then reverses | Long wick beyond key level followed by immediate reversal |
| **Order block** | Last opposing candle before a big move | Last red candle before big green move = buy order block |
| **Premium** | Price above 50% of the range = expensive | Sell bias when in premium |
| **Discount** | Price below 50% of the range = cheap | Buy bias when in discount |
| **NDOG** | New Day Opening Gap | Gap between yesterday close and today open |
| **NWOG** | New Week Opening Gap | Gap between Friday close and Monday open |
